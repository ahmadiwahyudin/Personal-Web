About Me

Bridging Empirical Linguistics with Computational Tools

Having completed undergraduate and master's degrees in linguistics at Universitas Padjadjaran (UNPAD) Bandung, my scholarly trajectory integrates computational rhetoric, sentiment dynamics, and critical discourse models.

Currently advancing doctoral research at Universitas Negeri Jakarta (UNJ), my research concentrates on quantitative lexical modeling, computational linguistics, computational semantics, socio-linguistic analysis, and automated natural language analysis.
